Dog Recognition

Labrador Retriever
Medium to large size, short dense coat, broad head, floppy ears, and a strong, sturdy build.
Golden Retriever
Large breed with long golden fur, feathered tail, friendly eyes, and a balanced, athletic body.
German Shepherd
Large, muscular dog with a long muzzle, upright pointed ears, and a thick double coat.
Border Collie
Medium size with a lean, athletic body, medium-length coat, and alert, expressive eyes.
Yorkshire Terrier
Tiny dog with long, silky, fine hair, small upright ears, and a compact body.
